Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours

One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, but free roam continues after the story — so do the chapter-gated things while you are in them, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Cheats disable trophies and saving: leave them off.

    Missable wall — read this before Chapter 1 ends

    PowerPyx and GTABase agree: only one trophy in the whole set can be missed. Story trophies cannot be missed — they pop by playing — and everything else stays available in free roam after the story. The wall below is the complete list.

    Safety net: manual save at the start of every chapter, one more before the final mission. If a prompt never appears, reload the chapter-start save.
